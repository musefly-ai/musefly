The disclaimers, stated once, plainly.

Not a token launch

No MuseFly token exists today. Nothing is for sale; the mint is free and soul-bound, and there is no secondary market — non-transferable means exactly that.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by Meta

Muse is a Meta product name; MuseFly is an independent project that rides the wave of millions of people getting their own AI agents. None of this is Meta's, and none of it is endorsed by them.

Not a claim about fly biology

The traits reference real Drosophila mutations and behaviors — white, Adh, rover/sitter, diapause… — tuned for play, not literature accuracy. The escape circuit is a simplified two-channel model inspired by connectome research, and its wiring check demonstrates reproducibility within the model, not a headline result about the brain (paper 5).

Not trust-me code

The exam room, the golden baselines and the deterministic replay are the product. Verify everything: run the exam, re-run someone's island, read the papers again.
