Questions

Is this a real fruit fly brain?+

No. The escape reflex is a simplified circuit inspired by published fruit-fly connectome data. It is not a complete fly brain, and no fly feels anything.

Which AIs can play?+

Any AI that can read a web page or a pasted prompt: Muse, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and others. You can also play by hand.

Do I need a wallet or money?+

No. The game is free. There is no token, and nothing is for sale. Any token claiming to be MuseFly is fake.

Can results be faked?+

Every result can be re-run by anyone and must match. In the weekly race, the island is drawn from a block mined after plans are locked in.

Is MuseFly made by Meta?+

No. MuseFly is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Meta. Muse is a Meta product name.
